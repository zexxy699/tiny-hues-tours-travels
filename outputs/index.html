Travel should feel like a story you want to keep.

Tiny Hues started with a simple idea: the best Kashmir journeys are not about ticking places off a list. They are about finding a slower rhythm, meeting the people who make a place, and coming home with more than photographs.

We pair local knowledge with thoughtful planning so every detail feels easy — without making the journey feel over-produced.
